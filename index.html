About

Born in Hunan, China, Chen Guojun received his bachelor's and master's degrees from the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, China, and his doctorate from Joongbu University, South Korea.

He writes poetry as well as code, seeing images as signs and signs as images. His practice explores how material forms are mapped into mental imagery, and how those forms are constructed in the physical world.

He works primarily with digital media, interactive installations and video games.

Works

01

Flight Matrix

Year 2025

A kinetic installation whose individual units move in rhythmic patterns under the control of microcontrollers (MCUs). Colour, form and movement are interwoven. The units take their form from wild geese; the work was also inspired by Zhurong, the god of fire.

02

Zero-Sum World

Year 2018

A digital work generated entirely through code, computed in real time and variable in scale.

It explores the ebb and flow of interdependent events within a larger whole.

03

Wandering in Art: Lorenz Attractor

Year 2024

A small interactive installation in which two game joysticks mounted on a painting stool control an LED matrix between them. Particular movements trigger ordered patterns of light flashing on and off.

04

Wang Xiaobo and Chen Qingyang

Year 2007–2019

This work explores reciprocal mappings between the physical, digital and inner worlds. Wang Xiaobo's fiction influenced me in my youth. Chen Qingyang, a protagonist in his fiction, an old computer I once used, and a game joystick form a three-way correspondence.

05

Poetry Shaped by Objects: Formal MachineIndividual unit

Year 2023

Part of the Poetry Shaped by Objects series. The work maps the forms of physical objects to characters, then presents these characters like poetry inside a coal passage in an abandoned factory in Shanghai.

06

Poetry Shaped by Objects: Buffer ZoneOverall installation

Year 2023

Part of the Poetry Shaped by Objects series. For a time, I held a strong conviction that everything was form—in the most radical sense of the word. I built a video game machine to embody this idea, using a discarded church pew as its cabinet.

07

Vector Space

Year 2017

An early interactive installation that runs a small artificial neural network (ANN) to respond to interaction from the outside world.

08

On a Rainy Day, I Made an AI Insect in Decaying Wood

Year 2021

I placed a digital insect, equipped with a simple artificial neural network (ANN), inside a discarded old tree trunk. Visitors interact with the insect using a game joystick. This is also, in essence, a video game work.
