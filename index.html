个人简介Artist biography

生于中国湖南，本科、硕士毕业于广州美术学院（中国），博士毕业于中部大学（韩国）。

写诗也写代码，将图视为符也视符为图，关注物象在意象中的映射，也关注物象在现实中的构建。

主要以数字媒体、互动装置，电子游戏等艺术载体进行创作。

Born in Hunan, China, Chen Guojun received his bachelor's and master's degrees from the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, China, and his doctorate from Joongbu University, South Korea.

He writes poetry as well as code, seeing images as signs and signs as images. His practice explores how material forms are mapped into mental imagery, and how those forms are constructed in the physical world.

He works primarily with digital media, interactive installations and video games.
